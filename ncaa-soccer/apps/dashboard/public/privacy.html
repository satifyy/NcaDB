College Soccer Lab

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

College Soccer Lab (“NCADB”, “we”) publishes NCAA Division I men’s soccer statistics at satifyy.com/ncadb. You can read every statistic without an account. If you create one, we keep only what it takes to save your teams, players and preferences. We do not sell your information or show ads.

Information we collect

When you browse without an account. Our hosting providers receive standard request data (IP address, browser user agent, the page requested, time). We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

When you create an account. Sign-in is provided by Clerk. Clerk holds your email address, your password (stored hashed, never by us) and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google shares (name, email address, profile picture). Our own database stores:

  • your Clerk user ID and the display name you choose;
  • the teams and players you save, and your site preferences;
  • an activity log of account actions (for example “favorite added” or “account deleted”), with the time, a request ID and the IP address of the request.

We do not copy your email address, password or Google profile into our database.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your saved teams, players and preferences.
  • To keep the service secure: rate limiting, abuse prevention, bot protection and investigating misuse.
  • To understand overall traffic, in aggregate.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

Cookies and storage

The statistics pages set no cookies. When you sign in, Clerk sets cookies that are required to keep your session. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the site, each acting on our behalf:

  • Clerk: accounts and sign-in;
  • Google: only if you choose “Sign in with Google”;
  • Vercel: hosting of the website;
  • Railway: hosting of the API and database;
  • Cloudflare: DNS, network delivery and privacy-friendly analytics.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the service and its users.

Retention and deletion

Your saved teams, players and preferences are kept while your account exists. You can delete them at any time from your profile page (“Delete my NCADB data”), which removes your profile, favorites and preferences immediately. The activity log is kept for security; after deletion its entries are no longer linked to a profile, but they still contain the Clerk user ID and IP address. Your sign-in account itself is held by Clerk; you can close it from the account settings page or by asking us, and we will delete it along with your remaining data.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Account data lives in a separate part of the database that the public statistics service cannot read, and no passwords are stored by us. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Significant changes will be noted on the site.
